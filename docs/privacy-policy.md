# ParkMax Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 22 August 2026**

ParkMax is published by HopeTree Foundation. This describes what the app does with your
information. It is short because the app does little with it.

There is no account, no sign-up and no password. We do not run a server, and nothing you
record is sent to us.

## What stays on your device

Everything you log lives on your iPhone:

- Parks you have found, and the ones you have marked as not being parks
- Visits, with their dates, notes, ratings, companions and any photos or video you attach
- Places you save to measure from — home, school, work
- Which areas of the map have been searched, and how complete each city or county is

## What goes to your own iCloud

If your device is signed into iCloud, the app mirrors that data to **your** private iCloud
storage, so it reaches your other devices and survives a lost phone. This is your iCloud
account, not ours: we cannot read it, and it counts against your iCloud storage. Photos and
video are the bulk of it.

You can stop this in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud, and delete what is already there
under Manage Account Storage.

## What you choose to share with friends

The app gives you a random six-character code. It is not derived from your name, email,
Apple ID or device, and it identifies nothing about you on its own.

If you enter someone's code, or give them yours, the following is published so they can see
it:

- The name you typed into Settings, and your code
- Your totals: parks visited, visits logged, cities, current streak
- Your progress in each place you have indexed
- Places you have marked as not being parks
- Your recent visits — park name, location, date, note, rating, and one photo or video per
  visit, reduced to a size suitable for a feed

**Please read this part.** Shared visits are stored in a database that any person signed
into iCloud can read, not only the people you gave your code to. Do not share visits whose
photos, notes or locations you would mind a stranger seeing. Nothing is shared until you
have set a name in Settings, and nothing about a visit leaves your device unless you have
used the Friends tab.

## Location

The app asks for your location while you are using it, to find parks near you and to work
out how far away things are. Your location is used on the device. Searches go to Apple Maps
in order to find parks, and are handled under
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Your location is never attached to anything published to friends except through a visit you
chose to share, which carries the park's location — not yours.

Declining location does not block the app. Set a home location by hand in Settings and
everything works from there.

## Photos and camera

Only used when you attach media to a visit, and only what you pick. The app does not read
your photo library otherwise.

## What we do not do

- No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting of our own
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- No tracking across apps or websites
- No third-party services beyond Apple's own iCloud and Maps
- No selling or sharing of anything with anybody

Beta builds distributed through TestFlight give Apple crash logs and basic usage figures
under [Apple's terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not ours.

## Children

ParkMax is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from
them.

## Deleting your data

- **On the device:** Settings → Data → Delete everything.
- **In iCloud:** iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → ParkMax →
  Delete Data. If sync is on, deleting in the app also removes it from iCloud, though it
  can take a few minutes to finish.
- **What you shared with friends:** removed when you delete the visits behind it and the
  app next syncs.
